About रसिका Stitch Works

Celebrating 15 Years of
Embroidery Excellence

रसिका Stitch Works was founded with a passion for preserving and promoting India's rich embroidery heritage. What started as a small home workshop has grown into an award-recognised name trusted by individual customers and businesses across India.

We specialise in hand-embroidered sarees, custom bridal blouses, suits & kurties, rangoli mats, wall art, and bespoke embroidery — each piece crafted with precision and love for the tradition.
